# German models and Intel acceleration — delta since September 2026

Verified 2026-10-06 against the live Hugging Face / PyPI / GitHub sources (URLs inline). Baseline: `2026-09-stt-engine-delta.md`. Only what is **new, changed or missed there** is listed. Nothing here was benchmarked on real hardware — every figure is from a model card, a vendor page or the source code.

## Conclusion

- **Accuracy:** nothing beats `primeline/whisper-large-v3-turbo-german` (shipped as `jimmymeister/whisper-large-v3-turbo-german-ct2`, avg WER 2.63 on primeline's mix).
- **Speed at German quality:** `primeline/parakeet-primeline` — a German fine-tune of Parakeet-TDT-0.6b-v3 by the same author, missed by the September pass — reaches avg WER 2.95 at Parakeet speed, and an onnx-asr export already exists. On an Intel laptop without NVIDIA this is the largest single lever: Parakeet runs ~30× realtime on a CPU where Whisper turbo runs ~3–4× ([onnx-asr benchmarks](https://istupakov.github.io/onnx-asr/benchmarks/)).
- **Why the German turbo is slow on Intel laptops today:** it has no OpenVINO IR (the app swaps it for plain `large-v3-turbo` on that backend), so it only runs through CTranslate2 on the CPU — with 4 threads (faster-whisper's `cpu_threads=0` → CT2 `intra_threads=0` → `min(4, hardware_concurrency)` unless `OMP_NUM_THREADS` is set, `src/utils.cc` v4.8.2), beam size 5 (the app default), and Whisper's fixed 30 s encoder window (`pad_or_trim`) however short the take.
- **No OpenVINO IR of any German Whisper fine-tune exists on HF** (searched `turbo-german`, `filter=openvino` + ASR tag, primeline's model tree). Converting it ourselves is standard and needs no new runtime dependency.

## C. Intel hardware

- **Arc iGPU (Core Ultra):** Intel publishes turbo INT8 numbers ([benchmarks](https://docs.openvino.ai/2026/about-openvino/performance-benchmarks/generative-ai-performance.html), data 2026-09-23) — Core Ultra 7-258V: first token 162–224 ms, ~6 ms/token; 7-255H: 267–338 ms, ~11–12 ms/token. No rows for Iris Xe, Meteor Lake, the NPU or the CPU.
- **NPU:** turbo on the NPU stays unverified and risky — [#1965](https://github.com/openvinotoolkit/openvino.genai/issues/1965) (turbo hang on Lunar Lake, closed without a documented fix), #4222 (whisper-small fails on Lunar Lake, 2026.2.1), #3533 (`initial_prompt` blocked on NPU). Driver ≥ 32.0.100.3104. The community `FluidInference/*-ov-npu` repos are stateless exports and are not expected to load on 2026.4+.
- **Hybrid CPUs:** Intel's guidance for compute-bound math is P-cores only ([oneMKL heterogeneous cores](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/docs/onemkl/developer-guide-linux/2025-0/managing-performance-with-heterogeneous-cores.html)) — relevant to a `cpu_threads` default.

## D. Converting the German turbo to OpenVINO IR

- Template: [OpenVINO/whisper-large-v3-turbo-int8-ov](https://huggingface.co/OpenVINO/whisper-large-v3-turbo-int8-ov) — `openvino_{encoder,decoder}_model`, `openvino_{tokenizer,detokenizer}` (.xml/.bin), `openvino_config.json`, `generation_config.json`, tokenizer/preprocessor JSONs; the int4 sibling uses INT4_ASYM, ratio 1.0, group size 128.
- Recipe: `optimum-intel==2.2.0`, `openvino==2026.4.1`, `openvino-tokenizers==2026.4.1.0`, `nncf==3.4.0`, `transformers>=5.0,<5.6`, CPU-only torch; `optimum-cli export openvino --model primeline/whisper-large-v3-turbo-german --weight-format int8 out` (repeat for `fp16`, and `int4 --ratio 1.0 --group-size 128`). **Never `--disable-stateful`** (unsupported since 2026.4.0). NPU: FP16 or INT8.
- CI: a public-repo `ubuntu-latest` runner (4 CPU, 16 GB RAM, 14 GB SSD) should fit — estimated 6–10 GB peak RAM, 10–20 min per precision (not measured). Upload with `hf upload` and an `HF_TOKEN` secret, `WhisperPipeline` CPU smoke test in the same job.
- Apache-2.0 §4: ship the license text, state the files were converted and quantized, keep the attribution (primeline has no NOTICE file).
- App side: `openvino_model_repo()` maps the German preset to the new repo, and `OPENVINO_UNSUPPORTED_MODELS` loses its entry.
